# Korako Yolawani Community Code of Conduct

Korako Yolawani welcomes rigorous, good-faith participation from people with different backgrounds, disciplines and levels of experience.

## Our standard

We expect participants to:

- discuss ideas, evidence and technical claims rather than attack people;
- distinguish verified facts, hypotheses and opinions;
- give specific, falsifiable criticism where possible;
- respect privacy, intellectual-property boundaries and security-sensitive information;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, impersonation and deliberately deceptive behavior;
- avoid publishing credentials, private data, private-core implementation details or vulnerability information that should be reported privately;
- respect maintainer decisions about scope, moderation and the public/private boundary.

Disagreement is welcome. Personal abuse is not.

## Evidence and claim discipline

Korako uses the distinction:

```text
PREPARED != EXECUTED != VERIFIED != PUBLICLY REPRODUCED
```

Community contributions should not upgrade a claim beyond the evidence available. Synthetic tests, prototypes and internal validation should be described as such.

## Security and sensitive information

Do not post suspected vulnerabilities, credentials, personal data, private operational details or security-sensitive implementation information in public Discussions or Issues. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content, limit participation, or block accounts when needed to protect participants, the project or its security/IP boundaries.

Moderation decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and may consider repeated conduct, severity, intent and impact.

## Scope

This code applies to project-controlled community spaces, including Issues, Pull Requests and Discussions, and to project representation in related public collaboration spaces.
